KALLAWAY GRAMMAR (measured on his 20 latest reels, research/kallaway-20.md):
- Hook = the first sentence, 3.2-4.2 s (median 3.6 s): "Here's how to ...", "This is the ultimate ...", "These are the only N ...".
  The header types on with the hook; SFX are densest here (~26/min vs ~17/min in the body).
- Body: a named framework (The X Method / N things) told in 1-2-3 steps; each step = a chapter or a list row revealed on the word.
- CTA: "Comment 'WORD' to get ..." 5-8 s before the end (19/20 reels) — only if the speaker actually says one.
- Layout: split panel ~71 % of the time, full-screen speaker ~29 % in runs of 2-5 s (median 3.0 s), one every ~10 s (7-14 s);
  full-screen graphics are rare (2/20 reels). About 9 layout switches per minute.
- The panel never sits still: something changes every ~1 s (58 changes/min); one hero object per 1.5-3.5 s beat.
- Look: near-black grid panel (sometimes light grid paper), bold sans headers (2 lines max) with a serif-italic accent word
  and a red highlight box or underline; dark cards with thin white borders; thumbnails/collages, phone mockups,
  documents with coloured highlighter lines, numbered rows with blurred (unrevealed) text, orbits of cards around a red pill,
  branch trees, radar rings, counters. Seam caption 1-3 words, white bold; quoted/important words in serif italic.
- Sound: a soft music bed ~17 dB under the voice (128-130 BPM, bright, "digital-sweet"); ~18 SFX/min synced to picture:
  53 % tiny pops/ticks (counters, rows, text), 22 % light risers (~4/min, peaking ON a reveal/cut), 13 % low swells,
  8 % glows/shimmers; the same sound almost never twice in a row.
